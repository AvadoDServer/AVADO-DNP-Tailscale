AVADO Tailscale

Checking Tailscale…

  1. Connect this AVADO

    Paste a reusable key from Tailscale → Settings → Keys.

    Get a key

  2. Approve its routes

    At avado, choose Edit route settings. Tick and Use as exit node.

    Open Machines

    Your Tailscale devices can then also reach the AVADO's login. If its password is still avado, change it (how).

  3. Add the AVADO as DNS

    Add nameserver → Custom: . Turn on Override DNS servers.

    Open DNS
  4. Connect your phone or laptop

    Install Tailscale, log in with the same account, press Connect.

    Get the app
  5. Open my.ava.do
    Open my.ava.do

More options

Log in with a link instead of a key

Open the Tailscale login page or scan the code with your phone.

Only you should open it: whoever logs in adds this AVADO to their Tailscale.

DNS only for my.ava.do

Instead of Override DNS servers, you can switch on Restrict to domain and enter my.ava.do. Your devices then keep their own DNS for everything else.

Linux computers

Connect with sudo tailscale up --accept-routes.

Two AVADOs in one Tailscale account

Both offer the same addresses, so only one works at a time. In Machines, untick the routes of the AVADO you do not need from afar.

Who can reach this AVADO

Every device in your Tailscale account, the same as a device at home, including the AVADO's login (SSH). Add only your own devices.

To change the AVADO's password (it starts as avado): on a computer with Tailscale on, open a terminal and type ssh avado@172.33.0.1. Log in with the current password, type passwd and choose a new one. Keep it safe: AVADO support asks for it when they help you. Not sure? Write to support@ava.do.

AI Connect through Tailscale

Your AI assistant can reach AVADO AI Connect at a secure address. It still needs one of your AI Connect keys.

This gives only AI Connect's door a public address; only someone with one of your AI Connect keys gets in. Your Admin and apps stay private. Switch it off when you do not need it.

Disconnect

Removes this AVADO from your Tailscale. You can connect it again at any time.

Tailscale connects your devices directly and encrypts the traffic. It does not pass through AVADO.
