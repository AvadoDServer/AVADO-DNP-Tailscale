Reach your AVADO from anywhere, for free

Tailscale is a free service that links your own devices (phone, laptop, AVADO) into a private network, wherever they are. AVADO Remote Access keeps working as before; this is an extra way in.

  1. Make a free account at tailscale.com (you can sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub).
  2. Install the Tailscale app on your phone or laptop and log in with the same account.
  3. Press the button below and log in once more to add your AVADO.

Open this link and log in with your Tailscale account. On a phone, scan the code.

Open the AVADO Admin from anywhere

Two one-time settings in your Tailscale account (login.tailscale.com/admin):

  1. Machines: open avado, choose Edit route settings and tick 172.33.0.0/16.
  2. DNS: under Nameservers choose Add nameserver → Custom, enter 172.33.1.2, switch on Restrict to domain and enter my.ava.do.

Then, with Tailscale on, http://my.ava.do opens your Admin on your phone or laptop.

AI Connect through Tailscale

If you use AVADO AI Connect, your AI assistant can reach it through Tailscale with a secure address. Your assistant still needs one of your AI Connect keys.

The first time, Tailscale may ask you to allow “HTTPS certificates” in your account; this page shows the link when it does.

ChatGPT and Claude on the web run on the internet and cannot see your home network. This switch gives AI Connect a public address. What it shares: only the door your assistant knocks on, and only someone with one of your AI Connect keys can get in. Your Admin, your apps, the AI Connect key page and the rest of your AVADO are not shared. Anyone on the internet can see that the address exists. Switch it off when you do not need it. Off by default.

Disconnect

Removes this AVADO from your Tailscale. You can add it again at any time.

Tailscale connects your devices directly and encrypts the traffic between them. Tailscale's servers see your devices' names and addresses so they can introduce them to each other; your traffic does not pass through AVADO.
