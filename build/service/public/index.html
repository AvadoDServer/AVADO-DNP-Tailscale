Reach your AVADO from anywhere, for free

Tailscale is a free service that links your own devices (phone, laptop, AVADO) into a private network, wherever they are. Once it is set up, the AVADO Admin and all your apps open on your phone or laptop just like at home. AVADO Remote Access keeps working as before; this is an extra way in.

  1. Make a free account at tailscale.com (you can sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub).
  2. Install the Tailscale app on your phone or laptop and log in with the same account.
  3. Press the button below and log in once more to add your AVADO.

Or log in with an auth key

In your Tailscale account open Settings → Keys, press Generate auth key, copy the key and paste it here. The key is used once to add this AVADO and is not kept.

An auth key is saved in this app's settings in the Admin (Advanced mode: this app → Settings → Environment variables → TS_AUTHKEY). The AVADO uses it by itself whenever it is not logged in yet.

Open the AVADO Admin and your apps from anywhere

Three one-time steps in your Tailscale account, then two on your phone or laptop:

  1. Allow the AVADO network. Open Machines, find avado, open its ⋯ menu, choose Edit route settings, tick 172.33.0.0/16 and save.
  2. Let your devices find my.ava.do. Open DNS. Under Nameservers choose Add nameserver → Custom, enter 172.33.1.2, switch on Restrict to domain (Split DNS), enter my.ava.do and save.
  3. Keep your AVADO logged in. In Machines, open the ⋯ menu of avado and choose Disable key expiry. Otherwise Tailscale logs the AVADO out after a few months.
  4. On your phone or laptop: install the Tailscale app from tailscale.com/download, log in with the same account and switch it on. (On a Linux computer, also allow subnet routes: tailscale set --accept-routes.)
  5. Open http://my.ava.do in the browser. Your apps open the same way as at home, for example http://tailscale.my.ava.do.

Who can open it? Every device in your Tailscale account, the same as a device on your home network. Only add your own devices, and do not share this AVADO with other people in Tailscale.

Two AVADOs in one Tailscale account? Both offer the same network, so only one of them can be opened through Tailscale at a time. Switch this off on the AVADO you do not need from afar (or untick its route in Edit route settings).

AI Connect through Tailscale

If you use AVADO AI Connect, your AI assistant can reach it through Tailscale with a secure address. Your assistant still needs one of your AI Connect keys.

The first time, Tailscale may ask you to allow “HTTPS certificates” in your account; this page shows the link when it does.

ChatGPT and Claude on the web run on the internet and cannot see your home network. This switch gives AI Connect a public address. What it shares: only the door your assistant knocks on, and only someone with one of your AI Connect keys can get in. Your Admin, your apps, the AI Connect key page and the rest of your AVADO are not shared. Anyone on the internet can see that the address exists. Switch it off when you do not need it. Off by default.

Use your home internet when you are away (optional)

When you choose your AVADO as exit node in the Tailscale app on your phone or laptop, all its internet traffic goes through your home connection, for example on a public Wi-Fi. Websites then see your home address, and it can be slower. Off by default. After switching it on, allow it once: Machines → avado → Edit route settings → Use as exit node.

Disconnect

Removes this AVADO from your Tailscale. You can add it again at any time.

An auth key is saved in this app's settings, so the AVADO logs in again by itself when it restarts. To stop that, first empty TS_AUTHKEY in the Admin (Advanced mode: this app → Settings → Environment variables).

Tailscale connects your devices directly and encrypts the traffic between them. Tailscale's servers see your devices' names and addresses so they can introduce them to each other; your traffic does not pass through AVADO.
